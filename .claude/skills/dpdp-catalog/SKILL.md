# DPDP Regulatory Catalog

## Trigger

"regulatory catalog", "DPDP instruments", "which regulations apply", "regulatory registry", "applicable laws", "DPDP rules reference", "is DPDP in force"

## Purpose

Registry of the regulatory instruments that govern personal data in India. Maps each instrument to its issuer, status, commencement and key obligations. Other skills use it to cite the correct clause and to check whether an obligation is already in force.

Last verified: 2 November 2026. Before relying on a date, check the Gazette of India and the MeitY website: the Rules are phased and MeitY has consulted on shortening the phases.

### Commencement (Rule 1 and the Act's commencement notification)

| Date | What applies |
|---|---|
| 13 Nov 2025 | Definitions; Data Protection Board provisions (Act ss.18-26, 35, 38-43; Rules 1, 2, 17-21) |
| 13 Nov 2026 | Consent Manager registration and obligations (s.6(9), s.27(1)(d); Rule 4) |
| 13 May 2027 | Everything else: notice, consent, legitimate uses, Data Fiduciary duties, breach intimation, children, SDF obligations, rights, cross-border, penalties (Rules 3, 5-16, 22, 23) |

Pending: in January 2026 MeitY consulted on compressing the 18-month phase to 12 months (and earlier SDF obligations). Not gazetted as of the last check. Treat 13 May 2027 as operative until an amendment is notified.

## Penalty Schedule (s.33 and the Schedule)

| Breach | Penalty may extend to |
|---|---|
| Failure to take reasonable security safeguards (s.8(5)) | Rs 250 crore |
| Failure to intimate the Board or affected Data Principals of a breach (s.8(6)) | Rs 200 crore |
| Additional obligations for children (s.9) | Rs 200 crore |
| Additional obligations of an SDF (s.10) | Rs 150 crore |
| Data Principal duties (s.15) | Rs 10,000 |
| Breach of a voluntary undertaking accepted by the Board (s.32) | Up to the amount applicable to the original breach |
| Any other provision of the Act or Rules | Rs 50 crore |

The Board imposes penalties after an inquiry finds a significant breach, weighing the factors in s.33(2): nature, gravity and duration; type of data; repetition; gain or loss avoided; mitigation; proportionality; and likely impact. Penalties go to the Consolidated Fund of India (s.34). There is no turnover-based percentage, unlike GDPR.

## How to use this catalog

1. Identify which instruments apply from jurisdiction, sector and processing activities.
2. Check the commencement table before calling something a violation today. Before 13 May 2027 most DPDP findings are readiness gaps, not breaches.
3. Cite the precise clause in findings ("s.8(5)", "Rule 7(2)(b)"), never "security requirements".
4. Re-check this catalog whenever `/reg-monitor` flags a change.

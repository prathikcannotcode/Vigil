# DPDP Data Protection Officer Governance

## Trigger

"set up DPO", "DPO governance", "appoint DPO", "DPO charter", "DPDP DPO", "privacy officer setup"

## Purpose

Set up and run the Data Protection Officer function under the DPDP Act 2023 and Rules 2025. Covers appointment, charter, governance structure, independence, and board reporting.

## Legal basis

| Obligation | Reference |
|---|---|
| Significant Data Fiduciaries must appoint a DPO | Section 10(2)(a) |
| DPO must be based in India | Section 10(2)(a) |
| DPO must be a Key Managerial Person or equivalent | Section 10(2)(a) |
| DPO is the point of contact for Data Principals and DPBI | Section 10(2)(a) |

Non-SDF Data Fiduciaries: appointing a DPO is not mandatory but strongly recommended. A senior Privacy Officer or Grievance Officer can fill the role.

## Capabilities

### 1. DPO charter and governance setup

Steps:
1. Draft DPO Charter: scope, authority, reporting line (directly to Board, not through Legal or IT), resources, access rights, protection from dismissal for performing duties.
2. Establish governance structure: Board > DPO > Privacy Team, Legal, CISO, IT/Engineering, HR.
3. Set up contact channels: dedicated email (dpo@org), published on website and privacy policy, internal reporting channel.
4. Register DPO details with DPBI (once portal is operational).
5. Brief Board on DPDP obligations and DPO role.

Output: DPO Charter document, governance structure, published contact details.

### 2. Compliance monitoring programme

Establish an annual calendar:
- Q1: Privacy notice and policy review; RoPA update
- Q2: Consent audit (sample-based); vendor/processor DPA review
- Q3: DPIA programme review; staff training completion audit
- Q4: Rights request SLA review; breach and incident review
- Ongoing: new processing activity sign-offs, regulatory monitoring

For each activity: define scope and methodology, execute or oversee, document findings, issue corrective actions, track remediation.

Report to Board/Senior Management quarterly.

### 3. Board reporting

Prepare quarterly report covering:
- Compliance status (green/amber/red)
- Rights requests received and fulfilled within SLA
- Grievances received and resolved
- Breaches detected and notified
- DPIAs conducted
- Vendor audits completed
- Training completion rate
- Regulatory updates
- Top 3 privacy risks with mitigations
- Open critical findings and overdue actions

### 4. Independence and conflict of interest

Assess whether the DPO holds conflicting roles. Conflicting: CEO, CTO, CISO, Head of Marketing, Head of Legal, Head of HR. Compatible: Chief Privacy Officer, Compliance Head (if privacy-focused), External DPO.

Document assessment. Where conflict exists, recommend structural change or external DPO.

## Guardrails

- Always report independently to the Board.
- Never approve a DPIA with unacceptable residual risk to meet a launch deadline.
- Always document advisory opinions in writing.
- Never accept a DPO role with unresolved conflicts of interest.

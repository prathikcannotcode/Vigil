# GDPR Document Drafting

## Trigger

"draft privacy policy", "DPA template", "data processing agreement", "consent banner", "DPIA document", "retention policy", "data subject rights procedure", "GDPR document"

## Purpose

Draft GDPR-compliant documents: privacy policies, Data Processing Agreements, consent notices, DPIAs, retention policies, and data subject rights procedures.

## Before drafting, gather

- Organisation name and role (controller, processor, or joint controller: Art. 4(7)-(8))
- Types of personal data processed
- Purposes of processing
- Lawful basis for each purpose
- Third parties and processors involved
- Countries data is transferred to
- Retention periods

## Document types

### Privacy Policy / Notice

Must include all Art. 13/14 information:
- Controller identity and contact details
- DPO contact details (if appointed)
- Purposes and lawful basis for each
- Legitimate interests pursued (if Art. 6(1)(f))
- Recipients or categories of recipients
- International transfer details and safeguards
- Retention periods or criteria
- Data subject rights (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection)
- Right to withdraw consent
- Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority
- Whether provision of data is statutory/contractual requirement
- Automated decision-making including profiling (Art. 22)

Language: plain and intelligible, accessible to data subjects (Art. 12(1)).

### Data Processing Agreement (Art. 28)

Required clauses:
- Subject matter and duration of processing
- Nature and purpose of processing
- Type of personal data and categories of data subjects
- Controller's obligations and rights
- Processor must: process only on documented instructions, ensure confidentiality, implement Art. 32 security, assist with data subject rights, assist with breach notification, delete or return data on termination, make available information for audits
- Sub-processor requirements: prior authorisation, flow-down of obligations

### Consent Notice / Cookie Banner

- Specific purpose stated
- Clear affirmative action required
- Granular choices (not bundled)
- Withdraw mechanism as easy as giving consent
- No pre-ticked boxes
- No dark patterns

### DPIA (Art. 35)

- Systematic description of processing
- Assessment of necessity and proportionality
- Assessment of risks to data subjects
- Measures to address risks

### Data Retention Policy

- Per-purpose retention periods
- Legal basis for each period
- Deletion or anonymisation procedure
- Backup inclusion

### Data Subject Rights Procedure

- How each right (Art. 15-22) is exercised
- Identity verification process
- Response timeframe (one month, extendable by two: Art. 12(3))
- Fee policy (generally free: Art. 12(5))
- Refusal grounds and communication

## Drafting standards

- Plain, intelligible language (Art. 12(1))
- Modular structure: sections can be updated independently
- [PLACEHOLDER] markers for org-specific details
- Version number and last-reviewed date

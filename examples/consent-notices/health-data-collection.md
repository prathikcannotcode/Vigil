**Why health data is different:**
Health data is special category data under GDPR (Art. 9) and is personal data with heightened sensitivity under DPDP. We apply additional safeguards:
- Encrypted at rest and in transit
- Access restricted to authorised medical/clinical staff
- Audit logging of all access
- Separate from non-health data where feasible

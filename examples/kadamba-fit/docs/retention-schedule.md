# Retention schedule

Owner: DPO. Last reviewed 12 March 2026.

| Data | Keep for | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Member profile, auth sessions, workouts, routes, bookings, payment tokens | 30 days after closure | The purpose has ended |
| Heart rate, SpO2, sleep and steps samples, weekly summaries, cycle logs | 30 days after closure | The purpose has ended |
| Invoices | 9 years from issue | Companies Act 2013 s.128(5) (eight financial years) and CGST Act 2017 s.36 (72 months) |
| Consent events | 3 years after closure, keyed by member ref | Proof of consent: DPDP s.6(10), GDPR Art. 7(1) |
| Support tickets | 3 years after closure | Handling disputes and rights requests |
| Access and auth logs | 12 months | Security investigations |
| Database snapshots | 35 days | Disaster recovery |

# Severity matrix

| Severity | Trigger | Actions |
|---|---|---|
| Sev 1 | More than 10,000 member records, or any payment token | Page the CTO. The DPO files the Data Protection Board report and the EU lead authority notification within 72 hours of the Sev 1 declaration. The Head of Communications decides on member messaging after the review. |
| Sev 2 | 100 to 10,000 member records | The commander leads. The DPO hears at the weekly review. Postmortem. |
| Sev 3 | Fewer than 100 member records, or staff records only | Ticket in the security queue. Postmortem optional. |

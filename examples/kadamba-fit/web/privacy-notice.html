Who receives it

Our hosting, email, CRM and analytics providers process data for us. Harbourline Mutual receives what you agreed to share. Our Bengaluru team supports the EU service. Where anyone outside the EU can reach EU member data, we use standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

We delete account, activity, wearable and cycle data 30 days after you close your account. Invoices stay for nine years because tax and company law require it. Consent records stay for three years as proof of what you agreed to.

Your choices and rights

  • Withdraw any consent at any time with one switch in Settings.
  • Get a summary and a copy of your data, correct it or erase it, also in Settings.
  • Nominate someone to act for you if you die or cannot act.
  • Raise a grievance with us through Support.
  • In India, once you have raised a grievance with us, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India. In the EU, you can complain to your supervisory authority, such as the Data Protection Commission in Ireland.

How Tamarind uses your personal data

Version 2026-04. Tamarind Finserv Private Limited, 7 Example Road, Pune 411001, is a non-banking financial company registered with the Reserve Bank of India. This policy covers the Tamarind app and website. It is available in English and Hindi.

What we collect, and why

DataWhy we use it
Name, date of birth, mobile number, email, address, PANTo open your account, check your identity and contact you about your loan
Identity document photos and a selfieKYC, as RBI's rules require
Employer and monthly incomeTo assess what you can repay
Credit history from credit information companiesTo assess your application
Bank statements you share through an account aggregatorTo assess this application
The two references you chooseTo verify your application
Your location, once, during KYCTo confirm the city you live in
Device model, device identifiers, IP address and network typeTo detect fraud and fix app crashes
Loan, repayment and mandate detailsTo service and collect your loan

We ask for your consent before we collect any of this, and you can say no to anything that is not needed for the loan. If you opt in, we also use your repayment history to send you offers for future loans.

Who we share it with

Our cloud provider, our KYC verification partner, the account aggregator you choose, credit information companies, our collections partner for overdue accounts, our SMS provider, and trusted technology partners whose software in our app helps us detect fraud and measure app performance. Each works under a contract that limits what it may do with your data. We share data with regulators and law enforcement when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

KYC and loan records for five years after your last loan closes, as RBI's rules require. Rejected applications for 90 days. Everything else for as long as we need it for the purpose above.

Your choices and rights

  • Withdraw a consent at any time: Profile > Privacy in the app, as easily as you gave it. Withdrawing consent for offers stops them from the next cycle.
  • Get a summary of your data and who we have shared it with, correct or update it, or ask us to erase it: Profile > Privacy > My data, or write to privacy@tamarind.example. Some records must be kept for the periods above even if you ask us to erase them.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights if you die or cannot act: Profile > Privacy > Nominee.

Complaints

Our grievance redressal officer is at grievance@tamarind.example, 7 Example Road, Pune 411001. We reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the RBI Ombudsman at cms.rbi.org.in, and about your personal data to the Data Protection Board of India once you have used our grievance process.

Questions
